Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Aura (“we”) helps small businesses in Kenya show their services and take bookings. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Customers: your name, phone number, optional email, and the service, date and time you book.
  • Business owners: your name, email, phone number, and the business details and photos you add.

How we use it

Your booking details are shared with the business you book, so they can serve you. We send booking and approval emails through EmailJS. We use Google Firebase to store data and run the service. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Only the business you booked with, and Aura’s administrators, can see your booking details. Business pages (name, services, prices, photos, hours) are public.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep data while the service is in use or as needed for the business to manage bookings. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal data at any time, as the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 provides. Message us on WhatsApp at +254 115 506 649.

Security

Data is stored with Google Firebase over encrypted connections, and access is limited by account rules. No online service is completely risk-free.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
